Privacy Policy

Last updated November 2, 2026

MySelf doesn't collect any data. There's no account, no analytics, no advertising and no MySelf server. Nothing you add to the app is sent to us or to anyone else.

Where your data lives

Your to-dos, labels, office tasks, notes, images, University, Job Study and Product Dev data are saved as files on your Mac, inside the app's own storage.

If you turn on sync in Settings › iCloud, the app saves them instead in a folder you choose, usually in your iCloud Drive. Apple's iCloud Drive then copies that folder between your Macs under your Apple Account, as it does for any of your files. MySelf has no access to your iCloud account beyond that folder, and Apple's own privacy policy covers iCloud Drive.

Settings such as appearance and which features are shown are stored on your Mac, and copied to that folder only when you choose to sync them.

Network requests

The Mac App Store version of MySelf makes no network requests. The version downloaded from GitHub checks once a day whether a newer release exists by loading the project's public GitHub releases page. That request carries no personal data, and you can turn it off in Settings › General.

When you open a link you saved, it opens in your web browser, which follows its own privacy policy.

Deleting your data

Delete any item in the app, or delete the app and its data together. Anything in your sync folder stays there until you delete the folder.

Children

MySelf collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue on GitHub.
