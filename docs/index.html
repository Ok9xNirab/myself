Fourier series — cheat sheet

Any periodic signal with period T can be written as a sum of sinusoids. The coefficients tell you how much of each frequency is present:

x(t) = Σ ck ejkω₀t
pythonCopy
import numpy as np def coeff(x, T, k, n=2048): t = np.linspace(0, T, n, endpoint=False) w0 = 2 * np.pi / T return np.mean(x(t) * np.exp(-1j * k * w0 * t)) # c_k

Privacy

It's about you. So it stays with you.

Your tasks, classes and late-night notes are some of the most personal data you have. MySelf keeps all of it in files you own.

Files you own

Every item is a small JSON file on your Mac, or in an iCloud Drive folder you pick to sync between your Macs. There's no MySelf server.

No account, no tracking

Open the app and start. No sign-up, no analytics, no ads. The only request it makes is a daily update check on GitHub, which you can turn off.

Works offline

On a plane, in a lecture hall with bad Wi-Fi — MySelf doesn't notice.

Instant, always

No loading spinners. Your data is already on your Mac, so pages open without waiting on a server.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need an account?

No. Download, open, and start using it. MySelf starts empty; to look around first, load the sample data from Settings.

Where is my data stored?

In files on your Mac. To sync between Macs, choose a folder in your iCloud Drive in Settings › iCloud; each item is saved there as its own small file. MySelf never sends your data anywhere else.

Can I hide modules I don't use?

Yes. Not at university? Turn University off in Settings › Features and it disappears from the sidebar, the Dashboard, Quick Open and the Go menu.

Does it support Bengali?

Notes are designed for বাংলা from the ground up, set in the system's SF Bangla with comfortable line spacing, alongside LaTeX and code blocks.

Is there an iPhone or Windows version?

MySelf is built for the Mac. There's no iPhone, iPad or Windows release, and sync works between Macs only.

Why does macOS say it can't verify MySelf?

MySelf is free and isn't notarized by Apple, which needs a paid developer account. The app isn't damaged, and you can read every line of its source. Allow it once in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Open Anyway, or run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Myself.app.

Is Workout available?

Not yet. Workout is coming soon; its page is in the sidebar but doesn't save anything yet.
