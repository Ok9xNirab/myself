TODOs

One list for everything that's due.

Your personal tasks sit next to the office tasks, assignments and product cards that have a date, grouped into Overdue, Today, Upcoming and No date. Tick any of them off right here.

  • Filter by label, priority or space, or show only Today or Upcoming

Office Tasks

Life and work, side by side — never tangled.

Work lives in its own space, where every task is high priority by design and carries a link to its ticket, doc or pull request, so a deadline at work never hides behind “buy milk.”

  • A reference link on every task, one click away
  • Open, overdue and done counts at the top
  • New office task from anywhere with ⇧⌘N
University

Your semester, mapped. Every class, deadline and exam.

Add your classes once with their days and times, and MySelf keeps the upcoming sessions in order. Track each syllabus topic by topic, count down to every exam, and keep assignments and notes for the semester in one place.

  • Upcoming classes with course codes, times and rooms
  • Exam countdown with how prepared you are
  • Syllabus completion per course
Job Study

Prep for interviews like you'll actually remember it.

Break prep into subjects and topics — algorithms, system design, behavioral — and tick a topic each time you revise it. MySelf counts the revisions, remembers when you last went over it, and marks it due again after a week.

  • Revision count and last-revised date per topic
  • Link notes to a topic, or start a new one from it
  • Drag topics into the order you'll study them
Product Dev

Your solo company, on a board you'll actually open.

Each product gets its own Kanban board. Drag cards from Backlog to In Progress, Review and Done, tag them, and see side projects next to the rest of your life — so they don't only happen on weekends you forgot to plan.

  • One board per product, with its own icon and colour
  • Priority, due date and labels on every card
  • Labels of its own, kept apart from your TODO labels

Notes

Write the way you think — in math, code, or বাংলা.

Notes are pages, sorted into categories. Type an equation and it renders. Paste code and it's highlighted. Write in Bengali and it's set with proper Bengali typography. Formatting is just markdown, with a / menu for everything else.

LaTeX that just renders

Inline or display equations, typeset as you write. Built for problem sets and formula sheets.

Code, tables and images

Highlighted code blocks, markdown tables and images you paste or drop in, with a zoomable viewer.

বাংলা, done properly

Bengali is set in the system's SF Bangla, so conjuncts and vowel signs come out right.

Linked notes

Type [[ to link one note to another. Hover for a preview, and see what links back in the side rail.

Reader mode and PDF

Privacy

It's about you. So it stays with you.

Your tasks, classes and late-night notes are some of the most personal data you have. MySelf keeps all of it on your Mac and in your own iCloud account.

Your data, your iCloud

Everything lives in a database on your Mac and syncs through your private iCloud database between your Macs. There's no MySelf server.

No account, no tracking

Open the app and start. No sign-up, no analytics, no ads. The Mac App Store version makes no requests of its own; the GitHub download only checks once a day for a newer version, which you can turn off.

Sync when you say

Leave sync automatic, or turn it off in Settings › iCloud and sync only when you choose File › Sync Now.

Works offline

On a plane, in a lecture hall with bad Wi-Fi — MySelf doesn't notice. Pages open without waiting on a server.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need an account?

No. Download, open, and start using it. MySelf starts empty; to look around first, load the sample data from Settings.

Where is my data stored?

In a database on your Mac, synced through your private iCloud database to your other Macs on the same Apple Account, with nothing to set up. You can sync only when you choose, or turn iCloud off, in Settings › iCloud. MySelf never sends your data anywhere else.

Can I hide spaces I don't use?

Yes. Not at university? Turn University off in Settings › Features and it disappears from the sidebar, the Dashboard, Quick Open and the Go menu.

Does it support Bengali?

Notes are designed for বাংলা from the ground up, set in the system's SF Bangla with comfortable line spacing, alongside LaTeX and code blocks.

I'm updating from 1.x. Is my data safe?

Yes. 2.0 is a new look on the same data: your todos, notes, books and boards carry over as they are and keep syncing with your other Macs.

Is there an iPhone or Windows version?

MySelf is built for the Mac. There's no iPhone, iPad or Windows release, and sync works between Macs only.

Mac App Store or the DMG?

They're the same app and sync the same data through iCloud. The Mac App Store version updates itself. The DMG from GitHub checks for a new release once a day and links it, and also shows Workout, which is still in progress.

Is Workout available?

Not yet. Workout is coming soon; in the DMG its page is in the sidebar but doesn't save anything yet.
